# Glossary (FR → EN)

The ubiquitous language of the project. Code and docs use the French term.

| Term (FR) | English | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| copropriété | condominium | Building whose ownership is split into lots, each combining a private part and a share of the common parts. |
| syndicat des copropriétaires | owners' association | Legal entity made of all owners; decides in the AG. |
| syndic | property manager | Executes AG decisions and manages the building; professional or volunteer (bénévole). |
| gestionnaire | building manager | The syndic's employee in charge of a portfolio of copropriétés. |
| conseil syndical | owners' council | Owners elected to assist and oversee the syndic. |
| AG (assemblée générale) | general meeting | Annual (at least) meeting where owners vote on resolutions. |
| lot | lot | Private part + share of common parts (expressed in tantièmes). |
| tantièmes / millièmes | ownership shares | Share of the common parts attached to a lot; used for votes and charge allocation. |
| appel de fonds | call for funds | Periodic request for owners to pay their share of the budget. |
| règlement de copropriété | condominium bylaws | Founding document defining lots, common parts and charge rules. |
